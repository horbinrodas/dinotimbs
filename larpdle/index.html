How to play

  1. Type an artist on the screen, pick the right one with VOL + / −, then press MARK (or Enter).
  2. A 10-second clip starts playing.
  3. Type the song title, step through the matches with VOL + / − and lock it in with MARK. (or Enter)
  4. Get it right and your streak goes up. Get it wrong, skip or give up and you lose a battery bar. Three strikes and it's game over.
